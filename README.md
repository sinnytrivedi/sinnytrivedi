# Hi, I'm Sinny 👋

**Physicist working at the meeting point of nanomaterials, data and science communication.**
Based in Dublin, Ireland.

I study how materials behave at the nanoscale, from thin films to carbon nanotubes, and I like turning messy experimental data into clear, defensible results. I'm now applying that experience to research impact, evidence and data-driven work.

---

### 🔬 What I work on

- **Carbon nanotube electro-optics:** a non-destructive method for probing the aspect ratio and anisotropy of multi-walled carbon nanotubes, using AC-field alignment and Brownian relaxation tracked by transmitted light (presented at Electroceramics XX, Novi Sad, 2026)
- **Nanoscale characterisation:** conductive AFM analysis for energy materials, including FeNiB electrocatalysts (*Sustainable Energy & Fuels*, 2024)
- **Thin-film nanostructures:** PhD research in materials physics, Gujarat University
